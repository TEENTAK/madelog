CONTACT SHEET — BEFORE THE FIREWORKS FADE
Create a cinematic live-action storyboard contact sheet for a 35-second vertical short film titled “Before the Fireworks Fade,” a subtle Japanese summer festival romance between two adult Japanese lovers in their mid-20s.
IMAGE / BOARD SPECIFICATIONS
A clean 3-column × 2-row grid containing exactly six distinct cinematic storyboard panels, read from left to right, top to bottom. Landscape-oriented overall board with six equally sized widescreen panels. Thin neutral gutters between panels. No decorative frame, no text, no captions, no panel numbers, no watermark. Each panel must depict a different story beat, not a variation of the same composition.
VISUAL STYLE
Cinematic realism, authentic live-action film stills, natural human anatomy, realistic skin and hair, physically accurate fabric folds, restrained romantic performances, natural depth of field, realistic night exposure, atmospheric practical lighting, sophisticated Japanese summer-night color grading. Deep indigo-blue evening shadows, warm amber paper lanterns, pale blue and navy yukata, golden and crimson fireworks. No anime, no illustration, no fantasy styling, no artificial beauty-filter appearance.
CHARACTER CONTINUITY
The same adult Japanese woman and the same adult Japanese man appear in all six panels.
The woman wears a pale blue yukata with small white floral patterns, a dark navy obi, and neatly styled dark hair with a subtle hair ornament.
The man wears a deep indigo-navy yukata with a restrained traditional obi and has short, naturally styled black hair.
Keep their facial features, apparent ages, hairstyles, body proportions, clothing colors, accessories, and relative heights consistent across all panels. Their expressions and poses change naturally according to the story. They are an established young couple, comfortable with each other but still quietly shy in romantic moments.
ENVIRONMENT CONTINUITY
One coherent Japanese summer festival, moving from a lantern-lit festival street to a goldfish-scooping stall and then to a nearby riverside fireworks viewing area. Authentic yatai stalls, glowing paper lanterns, summer festival crowds, a shallow water basin with goldfish, fragile paper scoops, a dark river, and distant fireworks.
The story progresses from blue-hour dusk to full night. The riverside is part of the same festival location, not a different city or unrelated setting.
PANEL 1 — FESTIVAL ARRIVAL / ESTABLISHING SHOT
Wide cinematic tracking composition on a traditional Japanese summer festival street at blue hour. The couple walks side by side through a softly crowded lantern-lit street. The woman glances toward a nearby stall while the man looks at her with a subtle affectionate expression. Warm lanterns contrast with the cool evening sky. Establish the festival atmosphere and their relationship without exaggerated romance.
PANEL 2 — THE FAILED GOLDFISH SCOOP
Medium close-up at a traditional goldfish-scooping stall. The woman concentrates on lifting a goldfish with a fragile paper scoop. The wet paper tears just as she is about to succeed, and the goldfish slips back into the shallow basin. She pauses with a disappointed, slightly embarrassed expression. The man beside her tries to suppress a laugh. The basin, water, fish, and torn paper scoop are clearly readable.
PANEL 3 — BRIEFLY SEPARATED
Medium shot in the same festival street, now darker and busier. Festival visitors pass between the couple, briefly obscuring them from each other. The man stops and turns back toward the woman; she looks for him through the moving crowd. Their gazes meet across a small gap. Natural crowd movement, clear spatial relationships, a subtle shift from playful energy to emotional anticipation.
PANEL 4 — HANDS MEET
Intimate cinematic insert shot of the man's hand reaching toward the woman. Her hand hesitates for a brief moment, then settles into his palm. Their yukata sleeves and fingers are rendered with realistic anatomy and natural fabric folds. Warm lantern bokeh in the background. The gesture is understated, emotionally sincere, and physically plausible. The image clearly communicates that their hands are now holding each other.
PANEL 5 — FIREWORKS AND EYE CONTACT
Romantic medium close-up two-shot at the riverside at night. The same couple stands side by side, still holding hands. A large golden firework blooms across the night sky, illuminating their faces with a brief warm reflection. The woman initially looks upward at the fireworks; the man quietly looks at her instead. She turns and catches his gaze. A larger golden firework begins to bloom behind them at that precise emotional moment. Their expressions remain natural, restrained, and intimate. This is the visual and emotional climax of the film.
PANEL 6 — AFTER THE FIREWORKS
Quiet wide rear view of the same couple standing at the riverside, hands still joined, the woman gently leaning her head against the man's shoulder. The fireworks have mostly faded. Faint golden reflections ripple across the dark river, with only a few distant sparks remaining in the sky. The festival lights are soft and distant. Leave generous negative space around the couple. The image should feel peaceful, intimate, and nostalgic rather than tragic.
CINEMATOGRAPHY AND LIGHTING
Use distinct framing for every panel: establishing wide shot, expressive medium close-up, crowd-framed medium shot, hand insert, emotional two-shot, and distant closing wide shot. Keep perspective and human scale realistic. Use practical lantern illumination, cool ambient night light, believable firework reflections, natural shadows, and consistent visual direction.
STORY READABILITY
The six panels must communicate this exact causal progression:
Festival anticipation → goldfish scoop fails → the crowd separates the couple → the man reaches for her hand and she accepts → fireworks illuminate their exchanged gaze → they remain together after the fireworks fade.
Prioritize readable action, visual storytelling, continuity, subtle romance, and emotional payoff over decorative beauty.
EXCLUSIONS
No extra panels, no duplicated shots, no additional protagonists, no costume changes, no sudden location changes, no inconsistent character faces, no malformed hands, no extra fingers, no floating props, no impossible contact, no exaggerated crying, no forced kiss, no text overlays, no subtitles, no logos, no watermarks.
